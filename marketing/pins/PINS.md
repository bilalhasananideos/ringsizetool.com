## Descriptions

**Ring size chart in mm**
Measure the inside of a ring you already own and read off your US, UK and EU size. US 4 to 12
here; every quarter size from US 3 to 15½ is on the full chart, free and with no download.

**US ring sizes in letters**
A US 7 is N½ in UK and Australian letters. If the shop only sells whole letters, go one up to O.
US 4 to 11 at a glance, and every half letter from A to Z+6 on the page.

**Printable ring sizer**
Print at 100%, check the square with a ruler, then measure a ring or your finger. A sizer printed
4% small reads almost a full size too tight, so don't skip the check.

## Before posting

- One pin a day or so, not all at once.
- Boards: "Ring size guide", "Engagement ring tips" (make them on the account first).
- No hashtags stuffing. Two or three at most, or none.
